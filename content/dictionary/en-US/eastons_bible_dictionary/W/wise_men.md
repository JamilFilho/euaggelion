# Wise men

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Wise_men

---

**Wise men**: mentioned in Daniel 2:12 included three classes,

1. astrologers,
2. Chaldeans, and
3. soothsayers. The word in the original (hakamim) probably means "medicine men. In Chaldea medicine was only a branch of magic. The "wise men" of Matthew 2:7, who came from the East to Jerusalem, were magi from Persia or Arabia
