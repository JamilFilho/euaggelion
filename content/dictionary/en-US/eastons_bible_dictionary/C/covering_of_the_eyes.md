# Covering of the eyes

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Covering_of_the_eyes

---

**Covering of the eyes** : Covering of the eyes Occurs only in Gen. 20:16. In the Revised Version the rendering is "it (i.e., Abimelech's present of 1,000 pieces of silver to Abraham) is for thee a covering of the eyes." This has been regarded as an implied advice to Sarah to conform to the custom of married women, and wear a complete veil, covering the eyes as well as the rest of the face.
