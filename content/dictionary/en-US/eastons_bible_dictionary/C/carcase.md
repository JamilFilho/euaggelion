# Carcase

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/carcase.html

---

**Carcase:** contact with a, made an Israelite ceremonially unclean, and made whatever he touched also unclean, according to the Mosaic law (Haggai 2:13; comp. Numbers 19:16,22; Leviticus 11:39).
