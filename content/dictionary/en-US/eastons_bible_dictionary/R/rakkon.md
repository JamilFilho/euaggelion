**Rakkon**

Rakkon A place upon the shore, a town belonging to Dan (Josh. 19:46). It is now Tell er-Rakkeit, 6 miles north of Joppa, on the sea-shore, near the mouth of the river `Aujeh, i.e., "yellow water." (See [517]KANAH.)
