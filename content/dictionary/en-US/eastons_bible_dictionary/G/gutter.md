# Gutter

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/gutter.html

---

**Gutter:** Heb. tsinnor, (2 Samuel 5:8). This Hebrew word occurs only elsewhere in Psalm 42:7 in the plural, where it is rendered "waterspouts." It denotes some passage through which water passed; a water-course.

In Genesis 30:38,41 the Hebrew word rendered "gutters" is *rahat*, and denotes vessels overflowing with water for cattle (Exodus 2:16); drinking-troughs.
