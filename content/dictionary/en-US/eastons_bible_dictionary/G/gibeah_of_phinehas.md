# Gibeah of Phinehas

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/gibeah+of+phinehas.html

---

**Gibeah of Phinehas:** (Joshua 15:57, R.V. marg.), a city on Mount Ephraim which had been given to Phinehas (Joshua 24:33 "hill," A.V.; R.V. marg. and Heb., "Gibeah."). Here Eleazar the son of Aaron was buried. It has been identified with the modern Khurbet Jibia, Joshua 5 miles north of Guphna towards Shechem.
