# Garnish

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/garnish.html

---

**Garnish:** overlay with stones (2 Chronicles 3:6), adorn (Revelation 21:19), deck with garlands (Matthew 23:29), furnish (Matthew 12:44).

In Job 26:13 (Heb. shiphrah, meaning "brightness"), "By his spirit the heavens are brightness" i.e., are bright, splendid, beautiful.
