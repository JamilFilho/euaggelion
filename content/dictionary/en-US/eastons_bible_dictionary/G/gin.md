# Gin

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/gin.html

---

**Gin:** a trap.

1. Psalm 140:5, 141:9, Amos 3:5, the Hebrew word used, *mokesh*, means a noose or "snare," as it is elsewhere rendered (Psalm 18:5; Proverbs 13:14, etc).
2. Job 18:9, Isaiah 8:14, Heb. *pah*, a plate or thin layer; and hence a net, a snare, trap, especially of a fowler (Psalm 69:22, "Let their table before them become a net;" Amos 3:5, "Doth a bird fall into a net [*pah*] upon the ground where there is no trap-stick [*mokesh*] for her? doth the net [*pah*] spring up from the ground and take nothing at all?")
