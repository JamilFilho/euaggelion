# Graven image

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/graven+image.html

---

**Graven image:** Deuteronomy 27:15; Psalm 97:7 (Heb. pesel), refers to the household gods of idolaters. "Every nation and city had its own gods...Yet every family had its separate household or tutelary god."
