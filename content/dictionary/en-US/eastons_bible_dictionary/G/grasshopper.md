# Grasshopper

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/grasshopper.html

---

**Grasshopper:** belongs to the class of neuropterous insects called Gryllidae. This insect is not unknown in Palestine.

In Judges 6:5; 7:12; Job 39:30; Jeremiah 46:23, where the Authorized Version has "grasshopper," the Revised Version more correctly renders the Hebrew word ('arbeh) by "locust." This is the case also in Amos 7:1; Nahum 3:17, where the Hebrew word *gob* is used; and in Leviticus 11:22; Numbers 13:33; Ecclesiastes 12:5; Isaiah 40:22, where *hagab* is used. In all these instances the proper rendering is probably "locust" (which see).
