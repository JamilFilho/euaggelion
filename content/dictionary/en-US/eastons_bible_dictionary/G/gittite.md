# Gittite

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/gittite.html

---

**Gittite:** a native of the Philistine city of Gath (Joshua 13:3). Obed-edom, in whose house the ark was placed, is so designated (2 Samuel 6:10). Six hundred Gittites came with David from Gath into Israel (2 Samuel 15:18,19).
