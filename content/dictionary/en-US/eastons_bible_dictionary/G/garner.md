# Garner

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/garner.html

---

1. Heb. 'otsar, a treasure; a store of goods laid up, and hence also the place where they are deposited (Joel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 32:27, rendered "treasury").
2. Heb. mezev, a cell, storeroom (Psalm 144:13); Gr. apotheke, a place for storing anything, a granary (Matthew 3:12; Luke 3:17).
