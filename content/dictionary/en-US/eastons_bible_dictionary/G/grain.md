# Grain

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/grain.html

---

**Grain:** used, in Amos 9:9, of a small stone or kernel; in Matthew 13:31, of an individual seed of mustard; in John 12:24,1 Cor. 15:37, of wheat. The Hebrews sowed only wheat, barley, and spelt; rye and oats are not mentioned in Scripture.
