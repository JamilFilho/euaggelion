# Greek

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Greek

---

**Greek**. Found only in the New Testament, where a distinction is observed between "Greek" and "Grecian". The former is (1) a Greek by race (Acts 16:1-3; 18:17; Romans 1:14), or (2) a Gentile as opposed to a Jew (Romans 2:9, 10). The latter, meaning properly "one who speaks Greek", is a foreign Jew opposed to a home Jew who dwelt in Palestine.

The word "Grecians" in Acts 11:20 should be "Greeks," denoting the heathen Greeks of that city, as rendered in the Revised Version according to the reading of the best manuscripts ("Hellenes").
