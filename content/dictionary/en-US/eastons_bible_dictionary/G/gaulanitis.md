# Gaulanitis

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/gaulanitis.html

---

**Gaulanitis:** a name derived from "Golan" (which see), one of the cities of refuge in the territory of Manasseh (Joshua 20:8; 21:27; Deuteronomy 4:43). This was one of the provinces ruled by Herod Antipas. It lay to the east of the Lake of Galilee, and included among its towns Bethsaida-Julias (Mark 8:22) and Seleucia.
