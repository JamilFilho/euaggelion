# Miamin

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/miamin.html

---

**Miamin:** =Mijamin, from the right hand.

1. The head of one of the divisions of the priests (1 Chronicles 24:9).
2. A chief priest who returned from Babylon with Zerubbabel (Nehemiah 12:5), called Mijamin (Nehemiah 10:7) and Miniamin (Nehemiah 12:17).
