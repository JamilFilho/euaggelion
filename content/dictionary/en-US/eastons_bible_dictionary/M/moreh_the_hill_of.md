# Moreh, the Hill of

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/moreh%2C+the+hill+of.html

---

**Moreh, the Hill of:** probably identical with "little Hermon," the modern Jebel ed-Duhy, or perhaps one of the lower spurs of this mountain. It is a gray ridge parallel to Gilboa on the north; and between the two lay the battle-field, the plain of Jezreel (which see), where Gideon overthrew the Midianites (Judges 7:1-12).
