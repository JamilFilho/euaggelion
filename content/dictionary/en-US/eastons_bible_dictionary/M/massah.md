# Massah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/massah.html

---

**Massah:** trial, temptation, a name given to the place where the Israelites, by their murmuring for want of water, provoked Jehovah to anger against them. It is also called Meribah (Exodus 17:7; Deuteronomy 6:16; Psalm 95:8,9; Hebrews 3:8).
