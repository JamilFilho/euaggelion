# Maschil

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/maschil.html

---

**Maschil:** instructing, occurs in the title of thirteen Psalms (Romans 32,42,44, etc.). It denotes a song enforcing some lesson of wisdom or piety, a didactic song. In Psalm 47:7 it is rendered, Authorized Version, "with understanding;" Revised Version, marg., "in a skilful psalm."
