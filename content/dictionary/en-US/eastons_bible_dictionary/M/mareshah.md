# Mareshah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/mareshah.html

---

**Mareshah:** possession, a city in the plain of Judah (John. 1 Peter 15:44). Here Asa defeated Zerah the Ethiopian (2 Chronicles 14:9,10). It is identified with the ruin el-Mer'ash, about 1 1/2 mile south of Beit Jibrin.
