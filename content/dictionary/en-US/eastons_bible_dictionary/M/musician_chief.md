# Musician, Chief

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/musician%2C+chief.html

---

**Musician, Chief:** (Heb. menatstseah), the precentor of the Levitical choir or orchestra in the temple, mentioned in the titles of fifty-five psalms, and in Habakkuk 3:19, Revised Version. The first who held this office was Jeduthun (1 Chronicles 16:41), and the office appears to have been hereditary. Heman and Asaph were his two colleagues (2 Chronicles 35:15).
