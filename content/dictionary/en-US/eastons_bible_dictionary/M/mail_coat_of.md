# Mail, Coat of

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/mail%2C+coat+of.html

---

**Mail, Coat of:** "a corselet of scales," a cuirass formed of pieces of metal overlapping each other, like fish-scales (1 Samuel 17:5); also (1 Samuel 17:38) a corselet or garment thus encased.
