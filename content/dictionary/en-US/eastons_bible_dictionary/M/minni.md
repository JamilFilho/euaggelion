# Minni

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Minni

---

**Minni**: only in Jeremiah 51:27, as the name of a province in Armenia, which was at this time under the Median kings. Armenia is regarded by some as = Har-minni i.e., the mountainous country of Minni. (See ARMENIA.)
