# Madmenah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/madmenah.html

---

**Madmenah:** ibid., a town in Benjamin, not far from Jerusalem, towards the north (Isaiah 10:31). The same Hebrew word occurs in 25:10, where it is rendered "dunghill." This verse has, however, been interpreted as meaning "that Moab will be trodden down by Jehovah as teben [broken straw] is trodden to fragments on the threshing-floors of Madmenah."
