# Meraioth

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/meraioth.html

---

**Meraioth:** rebellions.

1. Father of Amariah, a high priest of the line of Eleazar (1 Chronicles 6:6,7,52).
2. Nehemiah 12:15, a priest who went to Jerusalem with Zerubbabel. He is called Meremoth in 12:3.
