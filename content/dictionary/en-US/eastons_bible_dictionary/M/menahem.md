# Menahem

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/menahem.html

---

**Menahem:** conforting, the son of Gadi, and successor of Shallum, king of Israel, whom he slew. After a reign of about ten years (B.C. 771-760) he died, leaving the throne to his son Pekahiah. His reign was one of cruelty and oppression (2 Kings 15:14-22). During his reign, Pul (which see), king of Assyria, came with a powerful force against Israel, but was induced to retire by a gift from Menahem of 2 Kings 1,000 talents of silver.
