# Maneh

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/maneh.html

---

**Maneh:** portion (Ezekiel 45:12), rendered "pound" (1 Kings 10:17; Ezra 2:69; Nehemiah 7:71,72), a weight variously estimated, probably about 2 1/2 or 3 lbs. A maneh of gold consisted of a hundred common shekels (which see). (Comp. 1 Kings 10:17, and 2 Chronicles 9:16).
