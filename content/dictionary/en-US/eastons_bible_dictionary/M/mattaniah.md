# Mattaniah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/mattaniah.html

---

**Mattaniah:** gift of Jehovah.

1. A Levite, son of Heman, the chief of the ninth class of temple singers (1 Chronicles 25:4,16).
2. A Levite who assisted in purifying the temple at the reformation under Hezekiah (2 Chronicles 29:13).
3. The original name of Zedekiah (which see), the last of the kings of Judah (2 Kings 24:17). He was the third son of Josiah, who fell at Megiddo. He succeeded his nephew Jehoiakin.
