# Uz, The land of

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/uz%2C+the+land+of.html

---

**Uz, The land of:** where Job lived (1 Chronicles 1:1; Jeremiah 25:20; Lamentations 4:21), probably somewhere to the east or south-east of Palestine and north of Edom. It is mentioned in Scripture only in these three passages.
