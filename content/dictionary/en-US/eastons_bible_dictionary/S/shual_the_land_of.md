# Shual, The land of

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shual%2C+the+land+of.html

---

**Shual, The land of:** land of the fox, a district in the tribe of Benjamin (1 Samuel 13:17); possibly the same as Shalim (1 Samuel 9:4), in the neighbourhood of Shaalabbin (Joshua 19:42).
