# Shaphat

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shaphat.html

---

**Shaphat:** judge.

1. One of the spies. He represented the tribe of Simeon (Numbers 13:5).
2. The father of Elisha (1 Kings 19:16-19).
3. One of David's chief herdsmen (1 Chronicles 27:29).
