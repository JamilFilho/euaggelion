# Shoe

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shoe.html

---

**Shoe:** Of various forms, from the mere sandal (which see) to the complete covering of the foot. The word so rendered (A.V.) in Deuteronomy 33:25, *min'al*, "a bar," is derived from a root meaning "to bolt" or "shut fast," and hence a fastness or fortress. The verse has accordingly been rendered "iron and brass shall be thy fortress," or, as in the Revised Version, "thy bars [marg., "shoes"] shall be iron and brass."
