# Stones, Precious

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/stones%2C+precious.html

---

**Stones, Precious:** Frequently referred to (1 Kings 10:2; 2 Chronicles 3:6; 9:10; Revelation 18:16; 21:19). There are about twenty different names of such stones in the Bible. They are figuratively introduced to denote value, beauty, durability (Song of Solomon 5:14; Isaiah 54:11,12; Lamentations 4:7).
