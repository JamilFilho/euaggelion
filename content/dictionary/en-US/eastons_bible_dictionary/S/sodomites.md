# Sodomites

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/sodomites.html

---

**Sodomites:** those who imitated the licentious wickedness of Sodom (Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Kings 14:24; Romans 1:26,27). Asa destroyed them "out of the land" (1 Kings 15:12), as did also his son Jehoshaphat (1 Kings 22:46).
