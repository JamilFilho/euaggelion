# Shema

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shema.html

---

**Shema:** rumour.

1. A Reubenite (1 Chronicles 5:8).
2. A Benjamite (1 Chronicles 8:13).
3. One who stood by Ezra when he read the law (Nehemiah 8:4).
4. A town in the south of Judah (Joshua 15:26); the same as Sheba (Joshua 19:2).
