# Sibbecai

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/sibbecai.html

---

**Sibbecai:** the Lord sustains, one of David's heroes (1 Chronicles 11:29), general of the eighth division of the army (1 Chronicles 27:11). He slew the giant Saph in the battle of Gob (2 Samuel 21:18; R.V., "Sibbechai"). Called also Mebunnai (2 Samuel 23:27).
