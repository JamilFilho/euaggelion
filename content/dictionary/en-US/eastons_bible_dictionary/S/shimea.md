# Shimea

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shimea.html

---

**Shimea:** the hearing prayer.

1. One of David's sons by Bathsheba (1 Chronicles 3:5); called also Shammua (1 Chronicles 14:4).
2. A Levite of the family of Merari (1 Chronicles 6:30).
3. Another Levite of the family of Gershon (1 Chronicles 6:39).
4. One of David's brothers (1 Samuel 16:9, marg.).
