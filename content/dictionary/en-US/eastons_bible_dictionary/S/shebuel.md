# Shebuel

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shebuel.html

---

**Shebuel:** captive of God.

1. One of the descendants of Gershom, who had charge of the temple treasures in the time of David (1 Chronicles 23:16; 26:24).
2. One of the sons of Heman; one of those whose duty it was to "lift up the horn" in the temple service (1 Chronicles 25:4,5); called also Shubael (1 Chronicles 25:20).
