# Shomer

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shomer.html

---

**Shomer:** watchman.

1. The mother of Jehozabad, who murdered Joash (2 Kings 12:21); called also Shimrith, a Moabitess (2 Chronicles 24:26).
2. A man of Asher (1 Chronicles 7:32); called also Shamer (1 Chronicles 7:34).
