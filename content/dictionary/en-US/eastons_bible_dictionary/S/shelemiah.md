# Shelemiah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shelemiah.html

---

**Shelemiah:** whom Jehovah repays.

1. Ezra 10:39.
2. The father of Hananiah (Nehemiah 3:30).
3. A priest in the time of Nehemiah (Nehemiah 13:13).
4. Father of one of those who accused Jeremiah to Zedekiah (Jeremiah 37:3; 38:1).
5. Father of a captain of the ward (Jeremiah 37:13).
6. Jeremiah 36:14.
