# Shaphan

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/shaphan.html

---

**Shaphan:** a coney, a scribe or secretary of king Josiah (2 Kings 22:3-7). He consulted Huldah concerning the newly-discovered copy of the law which was delivered to him by Hilkiah the priest (2 Kings 8-14). His grandson Gedaliah was governor of Judea (2 Kings 25:22).
