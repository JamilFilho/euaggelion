# Kirjath-sannah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/kirjath-sannah.html

---

**Kirjath-sannah:** city of the sannah; i.e., of the palm(?), Joshua 15:49; the same as Kirjath-sepher (Joshua 15:16; Judges 1:11) and Debir (which see), a Canaanitish royal city included in Judah (Joshua 10:38; 15:49), and probably the chief seat of learning among the Hittites. It was about 12 miles to the south-west of Hebron.
