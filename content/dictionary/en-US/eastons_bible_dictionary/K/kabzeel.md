# Kabzeel

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/kabzeel.html

---

**Kabzeel:** gathering of God, a city in the extreme south of Judah, near to Idumaea (Joshua 15:21), the birthplace of Benaiah, one of David's chief warriors (2 Samuel 23:20; 1 Chronicles 11:22). It was called also Jekabzeel (Nehemiah 11:25), after the Captivity.
