# Kenath

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/kenath.html

---

**Kenath:** possession, a city of Gilead. It was captured by Nobah, who called it by his own name (Numbers 32:42). It has been identified with Kunawat, on the slopes of Jebel Hauran (Mount Bashan), Numbers 60 miles east from the south end of the Sea of Galilee.
