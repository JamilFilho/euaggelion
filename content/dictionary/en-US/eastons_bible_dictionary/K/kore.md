# Kore

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/kore.html

---

**Kore:** partridge.

1. A Levite and temple-warder of the Korahites, the son of Asaph. He was father of Shallum and Meshelemiah, temple-porters (1 Chronicles 9:19; 26:1).
2. A Levitical porter at the east gate of the temple (2 Chronicles 31:14).
3. In 1 Chronicles 26:19 the word should be "Korahites," as in the Revised Version.
