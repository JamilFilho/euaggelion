# Kartah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/kartah.html

---

**Kartah:** city, a town in the tribe of Zebulun assigned to the Levites of the family of Merari (Joshua 21:34). It is identical with Kattath (Joshua 19:15), and perhaps also with Kitron (Judges 1:30).
