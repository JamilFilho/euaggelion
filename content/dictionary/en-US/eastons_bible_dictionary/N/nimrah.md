# Nimrah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/nimrah.html

---

**Nimrah:** pure, a city on the east of Jordan (Numbers 32:3); probably the same as Beth-nimrah (Joshua 13:27). It has been identified with the Nahr Nimrin, at one of the fords of Jordan, not far from Jericho.
