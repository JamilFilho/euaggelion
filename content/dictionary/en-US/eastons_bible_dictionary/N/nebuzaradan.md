# Nebuzaradan

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/nebuzaradan.html

---

**Nebuzaradan:** "the captain of the guard," in rank next to the king, who appears prominent in directing affairs at the capture of Jerusalem (2 Kings 25:8-20; Jeremiah 39:11; 40:2-5). He showed kindness toward Jeremiah, as commanded by Nebuchadnezzar (Jeremiah 40:1). Five years after this he again came to Jerusalem and carried captive seven hundred and forty-five more Jews.
