# Nebaioth

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/nebaioth.html

---

**Nebaioth:** height.

1. Ishmael's eldest son (Genesis 25:13), and the prince of an Israelitish tribe (Genesis 25:16). He had a sister, Mahalath, who was one of Esau's wives (Genesis 28:9; 36:3).
2. The name of the Ishmaelite tribe descended from the above (Genesis 25:13,18). The "rams of Nebaioth" (Isaiah 60:7) are the gifts which these wandering tribes of the desert would consecrate to God.
