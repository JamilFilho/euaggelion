# Nethaniah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/nethaniah.html

---

**Nethaniah:** given of Jehovah.

1. One of Asaph's sons, appointed by David to minister in the temple (1 Chronicles 25:2,12).
2. A Levite sent by Jehoshaphat to teach the law (2 Chronicles 17:8).
3. Jeremiah 36:14.
4. 2 Kings 25:23,25.
