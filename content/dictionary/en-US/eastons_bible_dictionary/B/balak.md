# Balak

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Balak

---

**Balak**: empty; spoiler, a son of Zippor, and king of the Moabites (Numbers 22:2,4). From fear of the Israelites, who were encamped near the confines of his territory, he applied to Balaam (which see) to curse them; but in vain (Joshua 24:9).
