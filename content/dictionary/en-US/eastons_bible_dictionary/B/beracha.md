# Beracha

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Beracha

---

**Beracha** : Blessing. (1.) A valley not far from Engedi, where Jehoshaphat overthrew the Moabites and Ammonites (2 Chr. 20:26). It has been identified with the valley of Bereikut. (R.V., "Beracah.")

(2.) One of the Benjamite warriors, Saul's brethren, who joined David when at Ziklag (1 Chr. 12:3).
