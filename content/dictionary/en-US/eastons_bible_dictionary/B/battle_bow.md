# Battle-bow

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Battle-bow

---

**Battle-bow** : Battle-bow The war-bow used in fighting (Zech. 9:10; 10:4). "Thy bow was made quite naked" (Hab. 3:9) means that it was made ready for use. By David's order (2 Sam. 1:18) the young men were taught the use, or rather the song of the bow. (See ARMOUR, BOW.)
