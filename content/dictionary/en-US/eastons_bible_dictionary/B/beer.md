# Beer

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Beer

---

**Beer** : Well.

(1.) A place where a well was dug by the direction of Moses, at the forty-fourth station of the Hebrews in their wanderings (Num. 21:16-18) in the wilderness of Moab. (See [56]WELL.)

(2.) A town in the tribe of Judah to which Jotham fled for fear of Abimelech (Judg. 9:21). Some have identified this place with Beeroth.
