# Irha-heres

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/irha-heres.html

---

**Irha-heres:** according to some MSS., meaning "city of destruction." Other MSS. read *'Irhahares*; rendered "city of the sun", Isaiah 19:18, where alone the word occurs. This name may probably refer to Heliopolis. The prophecy here points to a time when the Jews would so increase in number there as that the city would fall under their influence. This might be in the time of the Ptolemies. (See ON.)
