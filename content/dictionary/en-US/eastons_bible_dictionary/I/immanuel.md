# Immanuel

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/immanuel.html

---

**Immanuel:** God with us. In the Old Testament it occurs only in Isaiah 7:14 and 8:8. Most Christian interpreters have regarded these words as directly and exclusively a prophecy of our Saviour, an interpretation borne out by the words of the evangelist Matthew (Isaiah 1:23).
