# Jephunneh

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jephunneh.html

---

**Jephunneh:** nimble, or a beholder.

1. The father of Caleb, who was Joshua's companion in exploring Canaan (Numbers 13:6), a Kenezite (Joshua 14:14).
2. One of the descendants of Asher (1 Chronicles 7:38).
