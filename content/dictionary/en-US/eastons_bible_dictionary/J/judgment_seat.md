# Judgment seat

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/judgment+seat.html

---

**Judgment seat:** (Matthew 27:19), a portable tribunal (Gr. bema) which was placed according as the magistrate might direct, and from which judgment was pronounced. In this case it was placed on a tesselated pavement, probably in front of the procurator's residence. (See GABBATHA.)
