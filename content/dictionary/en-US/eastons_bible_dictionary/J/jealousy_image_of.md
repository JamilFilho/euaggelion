# Jealousy, Image of

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jealousy%2C+image+of.html

---

**Jealousy, Image of:** an idolatrous object, seen in vision by Ezekiel (Ezekiel 8:3,5), which stood in the priests' or inner court of the temple. Probably identical with the statue of Astarte (2 Kings 21:7).
