# Jokneam

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jokneam.html

---

**Jokneam:** gathered by the people, (Joshua 19:11; 21:34), a city "of Carmel" (Joshua 12:22), i.e., on Carmel, allotted with its suburbs to the Merarite Levites. It is the modern Tell Kaimon, about 12 miles south-west of Nazareth, on the south of the river Kishon.
