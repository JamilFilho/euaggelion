# Joah

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Joah

---

**Joah**: Jehovah his brother; i.e., helper.

### Son of Obed-edom

[edit]

One of the sons of Obed-edom (1 Chronicles 26:4), a Korhite porter.

### Levite of Gershom

[edit]

A Levite of the family of Gershom (1 Chronicles 6:21), probably the same as Ethan (1 Chronicles 6:42).

### Son of Asaph

[edit]

The son of Asaph, and "recorder" (which see) or chronicler to King Hezekiah (2 Kings 18:18,26,37).

### Son of Joahaz

[edit]

Son of Joahaz, and "recorder" (which see) or keeper of the state archives under King Josiah (2 Chronicles 34:8).
