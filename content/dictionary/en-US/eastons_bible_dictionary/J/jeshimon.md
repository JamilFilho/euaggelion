# Jeshimon

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jeshimon.html

---

**Jeshimon:** the waste, probably some high waste land to the south of the Dead Sea (Numbers 21:20; 23:28; 1 Samuel 23:19,24); or rather not a proper name at all, but simply "the waste" or "wilderness," the district on which the plateau of Ziph (which see) looks down.
