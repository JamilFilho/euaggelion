# Judgments of God

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/judgments+of+god.html

---

1. The secret decisions of God's will (Psalm 110:5; 36:6).
2. The revelations of his will (Exodus 21:1; Deuteronomy 6:20; Psalm 119:7-175).
3. The infliction of punishment on the wicked (Exodus 6:6; 12:12; Ezekiel 25:11; Revelation 16:7), such as is mentioned in Genesis 7; 19:24,25; Judges 1:6,7; Acts 5:1-10, etc.
