# Jeshaiah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jeshaiah.html

---

**Jeshaiah:** deliverance of Jehovah.

1. A Kohathite Levite, the father of Joram, of the family of Eliezer (1 Chronicles 26:25); called also Isshiah (1 Chronicles 24:21).
2. One of the sons of Jeduthum (1 Chronicles 25:3,15).
3. One of the three sons of Hananiah (1 Chronicles 3:21).
4. Son of Athaliah (Ezra 8:7).
5. A Levite of the family of Merari (Ezra 8:19).
