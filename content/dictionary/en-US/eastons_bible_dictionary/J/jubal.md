# Jubal

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jubal.html

---

**Jubal:** jubilee, music, Lamech's second son by Adah, of the line of Cain. He was the inventor of "the harp" (Heb. kinnor, properly "lyre") and "the organ" (Heb. 'ugab, properly "mouth-organ" or Pan's pipe), Genesis 4:21.
