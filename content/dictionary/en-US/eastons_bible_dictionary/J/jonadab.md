# Jonadab

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jonadab.html

---

**Jonadab:** =Jehon'adab.

1. The son of Rechab, and founder of the Rechabites (which see), 2 Kings 10:15; Jeremiah 35:6,10.
2. The son of Shimeah, David's brother (2 Samuel 13:3). He was "a very subtil man."
