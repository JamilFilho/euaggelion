# Jared

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Jared

---

**Jared**: descent.

### Descendant of Seth

[edit]

The fourth antediluvian patriarch in descent from Seth (Genesis 5:15-20; Luke 3:37), the father of Enoch; called Jered in 1 Chronicles 1:2.

### Son of Ezra

[edit]

A son of Ezra probably (1 Chronicles 4:18).
