# Jediael

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jediael.html

---

**Jediael:** known by God.

1. One of the sons of Benjamin, whose descendants numbered 1 Chronicles 17,200 warriors (1 Chronicles 7:6,10,11).
2. A Shimrite, one of David's bodyguard (1 Chronicles 11:45). Probably same as in 12:20.
3. A Korhite of the family of Ebiasaph, and one of the gate-keepers to the temple (1 Chronicles 26:2).
