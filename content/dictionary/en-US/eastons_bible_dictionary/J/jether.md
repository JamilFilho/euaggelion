# Jether

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jether.html

---

**Jether:** surplus; excellence.

1. Father-in-law of Moses (Exodus 4:18 marg.), called elsewhere Jethro (which see).
2. The oldest of Gideon's seventy sons (Judges 8:20).
3. The father of Amasa, David's general (1 Kings 2:5,32); called Ithra (2 Samuel 17:25).
4. 1 Chronicles 7:38.
5. 1 Chronicles 2:32; one of Judah's posterity.
6. 1 Chronicles 4:17.
