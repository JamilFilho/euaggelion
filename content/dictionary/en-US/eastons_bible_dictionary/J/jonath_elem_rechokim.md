# Jonath-elem-rechokim

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jonath-elem-rechokim.html

---

**Jonath-elem-rechokim:** dove of the dumbness of the distance; i.e., "the silent dove in distant places", title of Psalm 56. This was probably the name of some well known tune or melody to which the psalm was to be sung.
