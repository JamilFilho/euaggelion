# Jehonathan

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jehonathan.html

---

**Jehonathan:** whom Jehovah gave.

1. One of the stewards of David's store-houses (1 Chronicles 27:25).
2. A Levite who taught the law to the people of Judah (2 Chronicles 17:8).
3. Nehemiah 12:18.
