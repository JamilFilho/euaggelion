# Jacinth

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jacinth.html

---

**Jacinth:** properly a flower of a reddish blue or deep purple (hyacinth), and hence a precious stone of that colour (Revelation 21:20). It has been supposed to designate the same stone as the ligure (Heb. leshem) mentioned in Exodus 28:19 as the first stone of the third row in the high priest's breast-plate. In Revelation 9:17 the word is simply descriptive of colour.
