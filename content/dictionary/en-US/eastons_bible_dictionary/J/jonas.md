# Jonas

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/jonas.html

---

1. Greek form of Jonah (Matthew 12:39,40,41, etc.).
2. The father of the apostles Peter (John 21:15-17) and Andrew; but the reading should be (also in John 1:42), as in the Revised Version, "John," instead of Jonas.
