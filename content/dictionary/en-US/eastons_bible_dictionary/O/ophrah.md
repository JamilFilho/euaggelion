# Ophrah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/ophrah.html

---

**Ophrah:** a fawn. 1 Chronicles 4:14.

1. A city of Benjamin (Joshua 18:23); probably identical with Ephron (2 Chronicles 13:19) and Ephraim (John 11:54).
2. "Of the Abi-ezrites." A city of Manasseh, John 6 miles south-west of Shechem, the residence of Gideon (Judges 6:11; 8:27,32). After his great victory over the Midianites, he slew at this place the captive kings (Judges 8:18-21). He then assumed the function of high priest, and sought to make Ophrah what Shiloh should have been. This thing "became a snare" to Gideon and his house. After Gideon's death his family resided here till they were put to death by Abimelech (Judges 9:5). It is identified with Ferata.
