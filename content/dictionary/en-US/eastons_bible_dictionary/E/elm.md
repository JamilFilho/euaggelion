# Elm

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/elm.html

---

**Elm:** Hosea 4:13; rendered "terebinth" in the Revised Version. It is the Pistacia terebinthus of Linn., a tree common in Palestine, long-lived, and therefore often employed for landmarks and in designating places (Genesis 35:4; Judges 6:11,19. Rendered "oak" in both A.V. and R.V.). (See TEIL TREE.)
