# Ezer

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/ezer.html

---

**Ezer:** treasure.

1. One of the sons of Seir, the native princes, "dukes," of Mount Hor (Genesis 36:21,27).
2. 1 Chronicles 7:21;
3. 1 Chronicles 4:4.
4. One of the Gadite champions who repaired to David at Ziklag (1 Chronicles 12:9).
5. A Levite (Nehemiah 3:19).
6. A priest (Nehemiah 12:42).
