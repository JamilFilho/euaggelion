# Eliphelet

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/eliphelet.html

---

**Eliphelet:** God his deliverance.

1. One of David's distinguished warriors (2 Samuel 23:34); called also Eliphal in 1 Chronicles 11:35.
2. One of the sons of David born at Jerusalem (1 Chronicles 3:6; 14:5); called Elpalet in 14:5. Also another of David's sons (1 Chronicles 3:8); called Eliphalet in 2 Samuel 5:16; 1 Chronicles 14:7.
3. A descendant of king Saul through Jonathan (1 Chronicles 8:39).
