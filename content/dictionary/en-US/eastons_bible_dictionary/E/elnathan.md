# Elnathan

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/elnathan.html

---

**Elnathan:** whom God has given.

1. An inhabitant of Jerusalem, the father of Nehushta, who was the mother of king Jehoiachin (2 Kings 24:8). Probably the same who tried to prevent Jehoiakim from burning the roll of Jeremiah's prophecies (Jeremiah 26:22; 36:12).
2. Ezra 8:16.
