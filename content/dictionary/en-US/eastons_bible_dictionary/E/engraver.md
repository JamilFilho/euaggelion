# Engraver

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/engraver.html

---

**Engraver:** Heb. *harash* (Exodus 35:35; 38:23) means properly an artificer in wood, stone, or metal. The chief business of the engraver was cutting names or devices on rings and seals and signets (Exodus 28:11,21,36; Genesis 38:18).
