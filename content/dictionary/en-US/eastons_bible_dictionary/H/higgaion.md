# Higgaion

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/higgaion.html

---

**Higgaion:** in Psalm 92:3 means the murmuring tone of the harp. In 9:16 it is a musical sign, denoting probably a pause in the instrumental interlude. In 19:14 the word is rendered "meditation;" and in Lamentations 3:62, "device" (R.V., "imagination").
