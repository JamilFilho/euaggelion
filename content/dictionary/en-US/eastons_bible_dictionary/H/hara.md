# Hara

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hara.html

---

**Hara:** mountainous land, a province of Assyria (1 Chronicles 5:26), between the Tigris and the Euphrates, along the banks of the Khabur, to which some of the Israelite captives were carried. It has not been identified. Some think the word a variation of Haran.
