# Hethlon

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hethlon.html

---

**Hethlon:** wrapped up, a place on the north border of Palestine. The "way of Hethlon" (Ezekiel 47:15; 48:1) is probably the pass at the end of Lebanon from the Mediterranean to the great plain of Hamath (which see), or the "entrance of Hamath."
