# Hem

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hem.html

---

**Hem:** of a garment, the fringe of a garment. The Jews attached much importance to these, because of the regulations in Numbers 15:38,39. These borders or fringes were in process of time enlarged so as to attract special notice (Matthew 23:5). The hem of Christ's garment touched (Matthew 9:20; 14:36; Luke 8:44).
