# Hammath

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hammath.html

---

**Hammath:** warm springs, one of the "fenced cities" of Naphtali (Joshua 19:35). It is identified with the warm baths (the heat of the water ranging from Joshua 136 degrees to 144 degrees) still found on the shore a little to the south of Tiberias under the name of Hummam Tabariyeh ("Bath of Tiberias").
