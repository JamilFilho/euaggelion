# Helam

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Helam

---

**He’lam**
—*place of abundance*—a place on the east of Jordan and west of the Euphrates where David gave a great victory over the Syrian army (2 Sam. 10:16), which was under the command of Shobach. Some would identify it with *Alamata*, near Nicephorium.
