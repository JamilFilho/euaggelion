# Hashabiah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hashabiah.html

---

**Hashabiah:** regarded by Jehovah.

1. Merarite Levite (1 Chronicles 6:45; 9:14).
2. A son of Jeduthun (1 Chronicles 25:3,19).
3. Son of Kemuel (1 Chronicles 26:30).
4. One of the chief Levites (2 Chronicles 35:9).
5. A Levite (Nehemiah 11:22).
6. One of the chief priests in the time of Ezra (Ezra 8:24).
7. A chief of the Levites (Nehemiah 12:24).
8. Ezra 8:19.
9. Nehemiah 3:17.
