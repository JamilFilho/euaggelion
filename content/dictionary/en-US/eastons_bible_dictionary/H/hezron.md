# Hezron

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hezron.html

---

**Hezron:** enclosed.

1. One of the sons of Reuben (Genesis 46:9; Exodus 6:14).
2. The older of the two sons of Pharez (Genesis 46:12).
3. A plain in the south of Judah, west of Kadesh-barnea (Joshua 15:3).
