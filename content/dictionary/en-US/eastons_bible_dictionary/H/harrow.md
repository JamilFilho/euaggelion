# Harrow

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/harrow.html

---

**Harrow:** (Heb. harits), a tribulum or sharp threshing sledge; a frame armed on the under side with rollers or sharp spikes (2 Samuel 12:31; 1 Chronicles 20:3).

Heb. verb *sadad*, to harrow a field, break its clods (Job 39:10; Isaiah 28:4; Hosea 10:11). Its form is unknown. It may have resembled the instrument still in use in Egypt.
