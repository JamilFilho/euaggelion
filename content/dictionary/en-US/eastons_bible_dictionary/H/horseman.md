# Horseman

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/horseman.html

---

**Horseman:** Heb. ba'al parash, "master of a horse." The "horsemen" mentioned Exodus 14:9 were "mounted men", i.e., men who rode in chariots. The army of Pharaoh consisted of a chariot and infantry force. We find that at a later period, however, the Egyptians had cavalry (2 Chronicles 12:3). (See HORSE.)
