# Hoof

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hoof.html

---

**Hoof:** a cleft hoof as of neat cattle (Exodus 10:26; Ezekiel 32:13); hence also of the horse, though not cloven (Isaiah 5:28). The "parting of the hoof" is one of the distinctions between clean and unclean animals (Leviticus 11:3; Deuteronomy 14:7).
