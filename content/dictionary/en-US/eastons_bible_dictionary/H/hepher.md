# Hepher

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hepher.html

---

**Hepher:** a well or stream.

1. A royal city of the Canaanites taken by Joshua (Ezra 12:17).
2. The youngest son of Gilead (Numbers 26:32; 27:1).
3. The second son of Asher (1 Chronicles 4:6).
4. One of David's heroes (1 Chronicles 11:36).
