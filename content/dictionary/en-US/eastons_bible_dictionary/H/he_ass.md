# He-ass

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/he-ass.html

---

**He-ass:** Heb. hamor, (Genesis 12:16), the general designation of the donkey used for carrying burdens (Genesis 42:26) and for ploughing (Isaiah 30:24). It is described in Genesis 49:14,2 Sam. 19:26. (See ASS.)
