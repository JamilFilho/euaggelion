# Heman

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/heman.html

---

**Heman:** faithful.

1. 1 Kings 4:31; 1 Chronicles 2:6, a son of Zerah, noted for his wisdom.
2. Grandson of Samuel (1 Chronicles 6:33; 15:17), to whom the 88th Psalm probably was inscribed. He was one of the "seers" named in 2 Chronicles 29:14,30, and took a leading part in the administration of the sacred services.
