# Hanan

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/hanan.html

---

**Hanan:** merciful.

1. A Benjamite (1 Chronicles 8:23).
2. One of David's heroes (1 Chronicles 11:43).
3. Jeremiah 35:4.
4. A descendant of Saul (1 Chronicles 8:38).
5. One of the Nethinim (Ezra 2:46).
6. One of the Levites who assisted Ezra (Nehemiah 8:7).
7. One of the chiefs who subscribed the covenant (Nehemiah 10:22).
