# Heber

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/heber.html

---

**Heber:** passing over.

1. Son of Beriah and grandson of Asher (Genesis 46:17; 1 Chronicles 7:31,32).
2. The Kenite (Judges 4:11,17; 5:24), a descendant of Hobab. His wife Jael received Sisera (which see) into her tent and then killed him.
3. 1 Chronicles 4:18.
4. A Benjamite (1 Chronicles 8:17).
5. A Gadite (1 Chronicles 5:13). (See EBER.)
