**Abihail**
father of might. (1.) (Num. 3:35). (2.) (1 Chr. 2:29). (3.) (1 Chr. 5:14).

(4.) The second wife of King Rehoboam (2 Chr. 11:18), a descendant of Eliab, David's eldest brother.

(5.) The father of Esther and uncle of Mordecai (Esther 2:15).
