**Abel-mizraim**
Meadow of Egypt, or mourning of Egypt, a place "beyond," i.e., on the west of Jordon, at the "threshing-floor of Atad." Here the Egyptians mourned seventy days for Jacob (Gen. 50:4-11). Its site is unknown.
