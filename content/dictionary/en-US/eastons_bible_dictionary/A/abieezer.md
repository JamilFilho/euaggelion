# Abieezer

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Abieezer

---

**Abieezer**, father of help; i.e., "helpful." (1.) The second of the three sons of Hammoleketh, the sister of Gilead. He was the grandson of Manasseh 1 Ch 7:18 From his family Gideon sprang Jos 17:2 comp. Jud 6:34 8:2 He was also called Jeezer Nu 26:30

(2.) One of David's thirty warriors 2 Sa 23:27 comp. 1Ch 27:12

(3.) The prince of the tribe of Dan at the Exodus Nu 1:12
