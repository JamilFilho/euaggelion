# Ammishaddai

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Ammishaddai

---

**Ammishaddai**,
people of the Almaighty, the father of Ahiezer, who was chief of the Danites at the time of the Exodus (Numbers 1:12; 2:25). This is one of the few names compounded with the name of God, Shaddai, "Almighty".
