# Azmaveth

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Azmaveth

---

**Azmaveth**

Strong as death.

(1.) One of David's thirty warriors (2 Sam. 23:31).

(2.) An overseer over the royal treasury in the time of David and Solomon (1 Chr. 27:25).

(3.) A town in the tribe of Judah, near Jerusalem (Neh. 12:29; |Ezra 2:24).

(4.) 1 Chr. 8:36
