# Abia

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Abia

---

**Abia**, my father is the Lord, the Greek form of Abijah, or Abijam Mt 1:7 instead of Abiah 1Ch 7:8 In Lu 1:5 the name refers to the head of the eighth of the twenty-four courses into which David divided the priests 1Ch 24:10
