# Aaronites

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Aaronites

---

**Aaronites**, the descendants of Aaron, and therefore priests. Jehoiada, the father of Benaiah, led 3,700 Aaronites as "fighting men" to the support of David at Hebron 1Ch 12:27 Eleazar Nu 3:32 and at a later period Zadok 1Ch 27:17 was their chief.
