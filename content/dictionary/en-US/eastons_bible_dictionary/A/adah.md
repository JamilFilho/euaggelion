# Adah

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Adah

---

**Adah**, ornament. (1.) The first of Lamech's two wives, and the mother of Jabal and Jubal Ge 4:19, 20, 23 (2.) The first of Esau's three wives, the daughter of Elon the Hittite Ge 36:2,4 called also Bashemath Ge 26:34
