# Abednego

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Abednego

---

**Abednego** servant of Nego=Nebo, the Chaldee name given to Azariah, one of Daniel's three companions (Daniel 2:49). With Shadrach and Meshach, he was delivered from the burning fiery furnace (Daniel 3:12-30).
