# Abda

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Abda

---

**Abda**, servant. (1.) The father of Adoniram, whom Solomon set over the tribute 1Ki 4:6 i.e., the forced labour (R.V., "levy").

(2.) A Levite of the family of Jeduthun Ne 11:17 also called Obadiah 1Ch 9:16
