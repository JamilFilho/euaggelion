# Azur and Azzur

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Azur_and_Azzur

---

**Azur and Azzur** : Helper.

(1.) The father of Hananiah, a false prophet (Jer. 28:1).

(2.) The father of Jaazaniah (Ezek. 11:1).

(3.) One of those who sealed the covenant with Jehovah on the return from Babylon (Neh. 10:17).
