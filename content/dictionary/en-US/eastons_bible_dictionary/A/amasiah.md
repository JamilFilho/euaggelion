# Amasiah

Fonte: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Easton%27s_Bible_Dictionary_(1897)/Amasiah

---

**Amasiah**,
burden of (i.e., "sustained by") Jehovah, the "son of Zichri, who willingly offered himself unto the Lord", a captain over thousands under Jehoshaphat (2 Chronicles 17:16; Compare Judges 5:9).
