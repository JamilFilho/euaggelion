# Toi

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/toi.html

---

**Toi:** a king of Hamath, who sent "Joram his son unto King David to salute him," when he "heard that David had smitten all the host of Hadadezer" (2 Samuel 8:9,10). Called Tou (1 Chronicles 18:9,10).
