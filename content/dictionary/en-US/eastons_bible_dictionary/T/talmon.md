# Talmon

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/talmon.html

---

**Talmon:** oppressed.

1. A Levite porter (1 Chronicles 9:17; Nehemiah 11:19).
2. One whose descendants returned with Zerubbabel to Jerusalem (Ezra 2:42; Nehemiah 7:45); probably the same as [1.].
