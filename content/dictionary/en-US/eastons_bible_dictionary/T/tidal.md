# Tidal

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/tidal.html

---

**Tidal:** (in the LXX. called "Thorgal"), styled the "king of nations" (Gen.14:1-9). Mentioned as Tudkhula on Arioch's brick (see facing page 1 Kings 139). *Goyyim*, translated "nations," is the country called Gutium, east of Tigris and north of Elam.
