# Tabret

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/tabret.html

---

**Tabret:** (Heb. toph), a timbrel (which see) or tambourine, generally played by women (Genesis 31:27; 1 Samuel 10:5; 18:6). In Job 17:6 the word (Heb. topheth) "tabret" should be, as in the Revised Version, "an open abhorring" (marg., "one in whose face they spit;" lit., "a spitting in the face").
