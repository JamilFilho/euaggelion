# Taanach

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/taanach.html

---

**Taanach:** a sandy place, an ancient royal city of the Canaanites, on the south-western border of the plain of Esdraelon, 4 miles south of Megiddo. Its king was conquered by Joshua (Matthew 12:21). It was assigned to the Levites of the family of Kohath (Matthew 17:11-18; 21:25). It is mentioned in the song of Deborah (Judges 5:19). It is identified with the small modern village of Ta'annuk.
