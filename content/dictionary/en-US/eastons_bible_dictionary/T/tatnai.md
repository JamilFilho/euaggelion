# Tatnai

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/tatnai.html

---

**Tatnai:** gift, a Persian governor (Heb. pehah, i.e., "satrap;" modern "pasha") "on this side the river", i.e., of the whole tract on the west of the Euphrates. This Hebrew title *pehah* is given to governors of provinces generally. It is given to Nehemiah (Isaiah 5:14) and to Zerubbabel (Haggai 1:1). It is sometimes translated "captain" (1 Kings 20:24; Daniel 3:2,3), sometimes also "deputy" (Esther 8:9; 9:3). With others, Tatnai opposed the rebuilding of the temple (Ezra 5:6); but at the command of Darius, he assisted the Jews (Ezra 6:1-13).
