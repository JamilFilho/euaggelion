# Tortoise

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/tortoise.html

---

**Tortoise:** (Heb. tsabh). Ranked among the unclean animals (Leviticus 11:29). Land tortoises are common in Syria. The LXX. renders the word by "land crocodile." The word, however, more probably denotes a lizard, called by the modern Arabs *dhabb*.
