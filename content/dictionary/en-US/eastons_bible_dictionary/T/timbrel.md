# Timbrel

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/timbrel.html

---

**Timbrel:** (Heb. toph), a small drum or tambourine; a tabret (which see). The antiquity of this musical instrument appears from the scriptural allusions to it (Genesis 31:27; Exodus 15:20; Judges 11:34, etc.) (See MUSIC.)
