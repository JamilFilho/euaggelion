# Timnath

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/timnath.html

---

**Timnath:** Genesis 38:12,14.

1. Heb. Timnathah, which is appropriately rendered in the Revised Version, Timnah, a town in Judah.
2. The town where Samson sojourned, probably identical with See Timnah [1] (Judges 14:1-18).
