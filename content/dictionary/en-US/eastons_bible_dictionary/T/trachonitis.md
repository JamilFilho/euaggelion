# Trachonitis

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/trachonitis.html

---

**Trachonitis:** a rugged region, corresponds to the Heb. Argob (which see), the Greek name of a region on the east of Jordan (Luke 3:1); one of the five Roman provinces into which that district was divided. It was in the tetrarchy of Philip, and is now called the Lejah.
