# Tree of the knowledge of good and evil

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/tree+of+the+knowledge+of+good+and+evil.html

---

**Tree of the knowledge of good and evil:** stood in the midst of the garden of Eden, beside the tree of life (Genesis 2,3). Adam and Eve were forbidden to take of the fruit which grew upon it. But they disobeyed the divine injunction, and so sin and death by sin entered our world and became the heritage of Adam's posterity. (See ADAM.)
