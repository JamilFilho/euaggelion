# Tola

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/tola.html

---

**Tola:** a scarlet worm.

1. Eldest son of Issachar (Genesis 46:13).
2. A judge of the tribe of Issachar who "judged" Israel twenty-three years (Judges 10:1,2), when he died, and was buried in Shamir. He was succeeded by Jair.
