# Taberah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/taberah.html

---

**Taberah:** burning, a place in the wilderness of Paran, where the "fire of the Lord" consumed the murmuring Israelites (Numbers 11:3; Deuteronomy 9:22). It was also called Kibroth-hattaavah (which see).
