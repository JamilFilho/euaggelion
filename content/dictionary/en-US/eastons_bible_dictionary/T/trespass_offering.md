# Trespass offering

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/trespass+offering.html

---

**Trespass offering:** (Heb. 'asham, "debt"), the law concerning, given in Leviticus 5:14-6:7; also in Numbers 5:5-8. The idea of sin as a "debt" pervades this legislation. The *asham*, which was always a ram, was offered in cases where sins were more private. (See OFFERING.)
