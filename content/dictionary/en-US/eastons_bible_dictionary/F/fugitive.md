# Fugitive

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/fugitive.html

---

**Fugitive:** Genesis 4:12,14, a rover or wanderer (Heb. n'a); Judges 12:4, a refugee, one who has escaped (Heb. palit); 2 Kings 25:11, a deserter, one who has fallen away to the enemy (Heb. nophel); Ezekiel 17:21, one who has broken away in flight (Heb. mibrah); Isaiah 15:5; 43:14, a breaker away, a fugitive (Heb. beriah), one who flees away.
