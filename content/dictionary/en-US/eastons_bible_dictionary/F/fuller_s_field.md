# Fuller's field

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/fuller%27s+field.html

---

**Fuller's field:** a spot near Jerusalem (2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 36:2; 7:3), on the side of the highway west of the city, not far distant from the "upper pool" at the head of the valley of Hinnom. Here the fullers pursued their occupation.
