# Fish-pools

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/fish-pools.html

---

**Fish-pools:** (Song of Solomon 7:4) should be simply "pools," as in the Revised Version. The reservoirs near Heshbon (which see) were probably stocked with fish (2 Samuel 2:13; 4:12; Isaiah 7:3; 22:9,11).
