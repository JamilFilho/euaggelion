# Freedom

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/freedom.html

---

**Freedom:** The law of Moses pointed out the cases in which the servants of the Hebrews were to receive their freedom (Exodus 21:2-4,7,8; Leviticus 25:39-42,47-55; Deuteronomy 15:12-18). Under the Roman law the "freeman" (ingenuus) was one born free; the "freedman" (libertinus) was a manumitted slave, and had not equal rights with the freeman (Acts 22:28; comp. 16:37-39; 21:39; 22:25; 25:11,12).
