# Fillets

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/fillets.html

---

**Fillets:** Heb. hashukum, plur., joinings (Exodus 27:17; 38:17,28), the rods by which the tops of the columns around the tabernacle court were joined together, and from which the curtains were suspended (Exodus 27:10,11; 36:38).

In Jeremiah 52:21 the rendering of a different word, *hut*, meaning a "thread," and designating a measuring-line of 12 cubits in length for the circumference of the copper pillars of Solomon's temple.
