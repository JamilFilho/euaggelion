# Perez-uzzah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/perez-uzzah.html

---

**Perez-uzzah:** the breach of Uzzah, a place where God "burst forth upon Uzzah, so that he died," when he rashly "took hold" of the ark (2 Samuel 6:6-8). It was not far from Kirjath-jearim (which see).
