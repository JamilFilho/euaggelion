# Pigeon

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/pigeon.html

---

**Pigeon:** Pigeons are mentioned as among the offerings which, by divine appointment, Abram presented unto the Lord (Genesis 15:9). They were afterwards enumerated among the sin-offerings (Leviticus 1:14; 12:6), and the law provided that those who could not offer a lamb might offer two young pigeons (Leviticus 5:7; comp. Luke 2:24). (See DOVE.)
