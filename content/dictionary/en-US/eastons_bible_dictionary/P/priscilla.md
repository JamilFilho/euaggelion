# Priscilla

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/priscilla.html

---

**Priscilla:** the wife of Aquila (Acts 18:2), who is never mentioned without her. Her name sometimes takes the precedence of his (Romans 16:3; 2 Timothy 4:19). She took part with Aquila (which see) in insturcting Apollos (Acts 18:26).
