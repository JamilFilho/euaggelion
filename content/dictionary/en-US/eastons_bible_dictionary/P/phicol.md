# Phicol

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/phicol.html

---

**Phicol:** great, the chief captain of the army of Abimelech, the Philistine king of Gerar. He entered into an alliance with Abraham with reference to a certain well which, from this circumstance, was called Beersheba (which see), "the well of the oath" (Genesis 21:22,32; 26:26).
