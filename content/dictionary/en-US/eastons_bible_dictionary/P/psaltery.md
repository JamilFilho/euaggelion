# Psaltery

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/psaltery.html

---

**Psaltery:** a musical instrument, supposed to have been a kind of lyre, or a harp with twelve strings. The Hebrew word nebhel, so rendered, is translated "viol" in Isaiah 5:12 (R.V., "lute"); Isaiah 14:11. In Daniel 3:5,7,10,15, the word thus rendered is Chaldaic, pesanterin, which is supposed to be a word of Greek origin denoting an instrument of the harp kind.
