# Parbar

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/parbar.html

---

**Parbar:** (1 Chronicles 26:18), a place apparently connected with the temple, probably a "suburb" (which see), as the word is rendered in 2 Kings 23:11; a space between the temple wall and the wall of the court; an open portico into which the chambers of the official persons opened (1 Chronicles 26:18).
