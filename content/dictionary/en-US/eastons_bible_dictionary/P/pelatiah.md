# Pelatiah

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/pelatiah.html

---

**Pelatiah:** deliverance of the Lord.

1. A son of Hananiah and grandson of Zerubbabel (1 Chronicles 3:21).
2. A captain of "the sons of Simeon" (1 Chronicles 4:42).
3. Nehemiah 10:22.
4. One of the twenty-five princes of the people against whom Ezekiel prophesied on account of their wicked counsel (Ezekiel 11:1-13).
