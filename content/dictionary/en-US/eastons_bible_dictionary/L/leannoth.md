# Leannoth

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/leannoth.html

---

**Leannoth:** for answering; i.e., in singing, occurs in the title to Psalm 88. The title "Mahalath (which see) Leannoth" may be rendered "concerning sickness, to be sung" i.e., perhaps, to be sung in sickness.
