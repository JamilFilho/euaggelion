# Left-handed

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/left-handed.html

---

**Left-handed:** (Judges 3:15; 20:16), one unable to use the right hand skilfully, and who therefore uses the left; and also one who uses the left as well as the right, ambidexter. Such a condition of the hands is due to physical causes. This quality was common apparently in the tribe of Benjamin.
