# Levirate Law

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/levirate+law.html

---

**Levirate Law:** from Latin levir, "a husband's brother," the name of an ancient custom ordained by Moses, by which, when an Israelite died without issue, his surviving brother was required to marry the widow, so as to continue his brother's family through the son that might be born of that marriage (Genesis 38:8; Deuteronomy 25:5-10; comp. Ruth 3; 4:10). Its object was "to raise up seed to the departed brother."
