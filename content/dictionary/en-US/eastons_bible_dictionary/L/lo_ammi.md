# Lo-ammi

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/lo-ammi.html

---

**Lo-ammi:** not my people, a symbolical name given by God's command to Hosea's second son in token of Jehovah's rejection of his people (Hosea 1:9,10), his treatment of them as a foreign people. This Hebrew word is rendered by "not my people" in 1:10; 2:23.
