# Lysias, Claudius

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/lysias%2C+claudius.html

---

**Lysias, Claudius:** the chief captain (chiliarch) who commanded the Roman troops in Jerusalem, and sent Paul under guard to the procurator Felix at Caesarea (Acts 21:31-38; 22:24-30). His letter to his superior officer is an interesting specimen of Roman military correspondence (Acts 23:26-30). He obtained his Roman citizenship by purchase, and was therefore probably a Greek. (See CLAUDIUS.)
