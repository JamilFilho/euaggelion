# Letter

Fonte: https://www.christianity.com/bible/dictionary/eastons-bible-dictionary/letter.html

---

**Letter:** in Romans 2:27,29 means the outward form. The "oldness of the letter" (Romans 7:6) is a phrase which denotes the old way of literal outward obedience to the law as a system of mere external rules of conduct. In 2 Corinthians 3:6, "the letter" means the Mosaic law as a written law. (See WRITING.)
